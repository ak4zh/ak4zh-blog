---
title: Your Kids Are Not Your Pets
date: 2024-06-02
---

One habit that reflects a disturbing trait among parents is treating their adult children as pets rather than independent individuals. They get themselves involved in every detail of their lives, from belief systems and food habits to even marriage decisions. Such controlling behavior is likely to have a deep impact on adult children's emotional well-being and autonomy.  

## Controlling Belief Systems  

The most insidious form of controlling has to do with imposing belief systems on adult children. Parents who have an inclination to impose their own ideologies without allowing room for independent thinking stifle the mental growth and critical thinking of the adult children. Adult children will feel trapped and have a difficult time developing their individual identity or set of values.  

## Food Habits and Lifestyle Choices  

Controlling parents tend to impose their way on their adult children, dictating what to eat and do. By imposing strict dietary restrictions or sets of guidelines for their lifestyles, parents prevent their children from making independent choices about their health and well-being. This can result in feelings of resentment, rebellion, or even disordered eating patterns as adult children struggle to assert their autonomy.  

## Marriage Decisions and Relationships  

Probably the biggest area of control has to do with marriage decisions and relationships. Parents who take to meddling in the romantic lives of their adult children, bullying them into conforming to definite expectations or preferences, damage the trust and mutual respect that is at the foundation of relationships. Interference will tend to lead to strained relationships, emotional turmoil, and a feeling of powerlessness over one of the biggest decisions of their lives.  

## Conclusion

In the final analysis, parents need to be sensitized to the fact that their adult children are not extensions of themselves but players with very different beliefs, choices, and paths to chart. By letting go of control and encouraging independence, parents are able to empower their adult children to excel, make sound decisions, and foster healthy relationships. **Remember, your kids are not your pets;** they are individuals with the right to respect, autonomy, and the space to choose how they want to live their lives.
